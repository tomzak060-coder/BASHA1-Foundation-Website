GET INVOLVED

Be part of practical compassion.

Meaningful community action takes people who are willing to give their time, skills, resources and support. There is a place for everyone who wants to contribute.

MAKE A CONTRIBUTION

Every helping hand can make a difference.

BASHA1 Foundation welcomes people and organizations who share an interest in responding to practical needs within communities.

You do not have to contribute in the same way as everyone else. Whether you have time to volunteer, professional skills to share, resources to contribute or connections that could support our work, your participation can help move an initiative forward.

Explore the different ways to get involved below.
