ABOUT BASHA1

Community centered work with people at the heart of it.

BASHA1 Foundation is a registered non-profit organization committed to practical compassion and community-centered action.

We exist to respond to real needs with real solutions, especially among children, students, widows and underserved families. Through community outreach, educational support and volunteer-driven initiatives, we turn compassion into practical action.

01

Our Mission

To uplift the lives of vulnerable individuals through charitable actions and community-driven initiatives.

02

Our Vision

To create a society where every child and vulnerable person enjoys equal opportunity, access to education, and a life of dignity, care, and hope.

OUR WORK

Where we have shown up.

Every project begins with a need. These are some of the communities and people BASHA1 has supported.

25 JUNE 2024

Ansuari-Deen Islamic School

Supported pupils with school uniforms and essential learning materials including books, pens and pencils.

18 AUGUST 2024

Nyohani Orphanage

Provided foodstuffs, drinks, biscuits and bottled water as part of a community care outreach.

IRSHADIYA Primary School

Supported pupils with school uniforms and learning materials to help improve their readiness for school.

2 APRIL 2025

Eid with the Community

Shared meals with people on the streets of Tamale and celebrated Eid with children at Tamale Orphanage.
